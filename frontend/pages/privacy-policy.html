Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 20, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Add On Communications collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information. It applies to all our services, websites, and customer-facing applications.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when subscribing to our services, creating an account, contacting customer support, or participating in promotions. This includes personal identifiers such as your full name, residential and billing address, email address, telephone and mobile numbers, date of birth for age verification where required, government-issued identification details (as required for KYC compliance under Indian regulations), and payment information including bank account details, credit/debit card numbers, and UPI identifiers.

We also automatically collect certain technical information when you use our services or visit our website. This includes your IP address, device type and model, operating system, browser type and version, approximate geographic location derived from your IP address, network connection type, website pages visited, time and duration of visits, channel viewing patterns for set-top box usage, broadband consumption data, service usage timestamps, and any crash reports or diagnostic logs from our equipment or customer applications.

Where you voluntarily provide sensitive personal information such as biometric identifiers, health-related information, or special category data through optional service features, we process this data only with your explicit consent and in accordance with applicable data protection laws. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our privacy team, though withdrawal may impact your ability to use features that depend on such data.

2. How We Use Information

The personal information we collect is used to deliver, maintain, and improve our core services. This includes processing subscription applications, activating and provisioning cable TV and broadband services, generating monthly billing statements, processing payments and refunds, dispatching technical personnel for installation and repair visits, authenticating your account when you contact support, and personalizing your channel recommendations or in-app experience based on your viewing history and stated preferences.

We also use your information for operational and administrative purposes such as conducting network performance analytics, detecting and preventing fraud, unauthorized access, or account takeover attempts, troubleshooting service outages or technical issues, sending transactional communications (billing reminders, service outage alerts, account security notifications), complying with legal and regulatory obligations including TRAI reporting requirements, and enforcing our Terms and Conditions or other contractual agreements.

With your explicit opt-in consent, we may use your contact information to send marketing communications about new Add On Communications product launches, special promotional offers, seasonal discounts, partner offers, and community events. You can opt out of receiving marketing messages at any time by following the unsubscribe link included in every email, replying STOP to SMS marketing, or updating your communication preferences in the customer portal.

3. Cookies

Our website and customer portal use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and collect information about how you interact with our online properties. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. We use essential cookies required for core website functionality (such as maintaining your login session and remembering your security preferences), analytical cookies that help us understand website traffic patterns and usage, and preference cookies that remember your settings such as language selection and display mode.

We also work with carefully selected third-party analytics providers (including Google Analytics) who set their own cookies to help us measure website performance and visitor behavior. These third-party cookies may collect information such as pages visited, time spent on site, geographic location, referral source, and device information. This aggregated, anonymized data helps us improve our website design, content relevance, and user experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to block cookies entirely, delete existing cookies, or notify you before a cookie is stored on your device. Please note that blocking essential cookies may prevent certain parts of our website from functioning correctly, including login functionality, payment processing, and secure account access.

4. Third-Party Disclosure

We may share your personal information with trusted third parties who perform services on our behalf under strict contractual confidentiality obligations. These service providers include payment processors and gateway operators who handle your subscription payments, local cable operator (LCO) franchisees who deliver last-mile services in your locality, courier and logistics partners who ship equipment or printed documents to your address, customer relationship management (CRM) platform providers, cloud hosting and infrastructure vendors, SMS and email delivery services, and professional advisors such as auditors, consultants, and legal counsel.

Under certain circumstances, we may be required to disclose personal information to governmental authorities, law enforcement agencies, courts, or regulatory bodies. Such disclosures are made only when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by applicable law, court order, statutory mandate, or legitimate government request, including compliance with TRAI directives, tax authorities' summons, or orders under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their commercial marketing purposes without your explicit written consent. In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of all or a portion of our business assets, your personal information may be transferred to the successor entity as part of the transaction, subject to the same privacy protections outlined in this policy or under customary confidentiality arrangements.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and organizational security measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include encryption of sensitive data at rest and in transit using AES-256 and TLS 1.3 protocols, role-based access controls restricting employee access to personal data on a strict need-to-know basis, regular security audits and vulnerability assessments of our systems and applications, network segmentation between customer-facing and internal systems, and secure offsite backup storage with encryption.

All payment card information is processed exclusively by PCI DSS Level 1 compliant payment processors. We never store complete payment card numbers on our own servers; instead, we retain only the last four digits for display purposes and a processor-issued token for subsequent recurring billing. Staff with access to personal data receive mandatory data protection and information security training on an annual basis, and are bound by confidentiality agreements and disciplinary policies for any unauthorized access or disclosure.

Despite these safeguards, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a personal data breach that poses a significant risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the appropriate regulatory authorities without undue delay, in accordance with applicable data breach notification laws and our internal incident response procedures.

6. Your Rights

As a data subject, you have certain rights regarding your personal information held by Add On Communications. You may request access to a copy of the personal data we maintain about you, request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information, request erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected (subject to legal retention requirements), and object to or restrict certain types of processing of your personal data.

Where you have provided personal data based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal. You may also request portability of your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format where technically feasible. If you are dissatisfied with our handling of your personal data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the appropriate data protection authority in India.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request by email to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@addoncommunications.net or by post to our registered office address. We will respond to valid requests within 30 days of receipt, subject to verification of your identity. Requests that are overly broad, repetitive, or manifestly unfounded may be rejected or subject to a reasonable administrative fee. We will provide clear written reasons for any rejection of a rights request.

7. Children's Privacy

Add On Communications services are designed for and marketed to adult subscribers. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal information from children under the age of 18 without verifiable parental or guardian consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without your consent, please contact us immediately at privacy@addoncommunications.net so that we can take steps to remove the information from our systems.

Where our services or features are made available to users under the age of 18, such access is provided solely through the subscription account of a parent or legal guardian who retains full responsibility for supervising the minor's usage and ensuring that content accessed is age-appropriate. Parental control features are available through the set-top box menu and customer portal to restrict access to mature-rated channels and content.

We encourage all parents and guardians to take an active role in monitoring their children's online activity and teaching them about safe internet practices. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal information directly from a child without appropriate parental consent, we will promptly delete such information from our active and backup systems.

8. Policy Updates

We may update or revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our processing practices, legal and regulatory requirements, industry standards, or business operations. When we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you through prominent means such as posting a notice on our website homepage, sending an email notification to the address associated with your account, or displaying an in-app notification the next time you access your customer portal.

Your continued use of Add On Communications services after the effective date of a revised Privacy Policy constitutes acceptance of those changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information on our privacy practices. This page will always display the current version of the policy along with its last updated date and, for significant changes, a summary of what has been modified.

For a period of 12 months following a material update, we will also maintain the immediately preceding version of this policy on request. If you have any questions about how this policy has changed over time, please contact our privacy team with your specific inquiry.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or our data processing practices, or if you wish to exercise your data subject rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer and privacy team using the contact information provided below. All privacy-related communications are handled confidentially by our dedicated compliance team.

You may reach our privacy team by email at privacy@addoncommunications.net, by phone on our regular customer care lines with a request to be transferred to the privacy team, or by writing to our postal address at 123 Mount Road, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600001, India, Attn: Data Protection Officer. We acknowledge receipt of all privacy-related inquiries within 5 working days and provide substantive responses within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with the outcome of our internal handling of your privacy concern, you have the right to escalate the matter to the appropriate supervisory authority in India. Our privacy team is available to provide guidance on the appropriate channels for escalation depending on the nature of your concern and your jurisdiction.
