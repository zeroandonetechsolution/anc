ABOUT ADD ON COMMUNICATIONS

Company Overview

Add On Communications Private Limited is a leading digital cable and broadband service provider focused on delivering high-quality entertainment, connectivity, and next-generation digital experiences across Tamil Nadu. As the first and largest full HD digital TV, Internet & Fiber Optic Network in the region, Add On Communications hosts a robust network that forms the basis of our Fiber Optic digital TV & Internet services to thousands of homes and enterprises.

Mission

To empower every home with seamless digital connectivity and HD entertainment.

Vision

The Digital Conduit for high-speed connectivity.

Values

Integrity, Customer-First, Innovation.

Our Journey of Excellence

  • 2014

    Founded with 50 LCOs

    Add On Communications began its journey with a vision to revolutionize digital entertainment, starting with 50 Local Cable Operators across Tamil Nadu.

  • 2018

    Launched HD Service

    Pioneered Full HD digital cable services in the region, bringing crystal-clear picture quality and immersive audio to thousands of households.

  • 2021

    Fiber Broadband Rollout

    Expanded our network infrastructure with cutting-edge Fiber Optic technology, delivering blazing-fast broadband internet to homes and businesses.

  • 2025

    Smart 4K Box Launch

    Introduced our next-generation Smart 4K Android TV Box with OTT integration, voice search, and Chromecast built-in for the ultimate viewing experience.

ANC

The Digital Conduit. Powering Indian homes with fiber internet, smart TV and premium entertainment since 2012.
