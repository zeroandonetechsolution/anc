FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services, packages, and support.

How do I get a new Add On Communications cable connection?

Getting a new connection is simple. You can either fill out our contact form on this page, call our toll-free number 1800-123-4567, or visit our head office on Mount Road, Chennai. Our team will schedule a site visit within 24-48 hours and complete the installation on the same day as the visit. Standard installation is free with any base package subscription.

What packages and plans does Add On Communications offer?

We offer four base packages: Free to Air (₹99/mo with 100+ FTA channels), Basic SD (₹199/mo with 150+ SD + 10 HD channels), Popular HD (₹349/mo with 200+ channels + 50 HD), and Premium HD (₹549/mo with 300+ channels, 100 HD, and built-in OTT apps). You can add broadcaster bouquets from Sony, Star, Zee, Discovery, and Viacom18 on top. Visit our Channel Packages page for the complete list and latest pricing.

How quickly can the installation be done?

We pride ourselves on fast installation. Within Chennai city limits, installations are typically completed within 24 hours of booking. For suburban and other areas, it may take up to 48 hours. Our technician will call you 30 minutes before arrival to confirm your availability. The entire setup process, including demo and activation, takes about 60-90 minutes depending on the package.

Does Add On Communications offer broadband internet as well?

Yes! Add On Communications offers high-speed fiber broadband plans starting at ₹499/month for 100 Mbps. Our broadband plans come with unlimited data, free Wi-Fi router on annual plans, and 24/7 technical support. Many of our customers bundle cable TV with broadband for exclusive combo discounts. Contact us for the latest broadband offers in your locality.

How do I pay my monthly bill?

We offer multiple convenient payment options. You can pay online through our customer portal, via UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm), by bank transfer, or set up auto-debit from your bank account or credit card. Cash payments are also accepted through our local cable operators (LCOs) and at our head office. All online payments come with an instant digital receipt sent to your email and SMS.

What is the complaint resolution process?

Every complaint logged through our support channels receives a unique ticket number. Signal issues and minor technical problems are typically resolved within 4 hours in urban areas and 8 hours in semi-urban areas. Hardware issues involving set-top box replacement are resolved within 24 hours. You can track your complaint status by replying to the ticket SMS or calling support with your ticket ID.

Can I change my package or add channels later?

Absolutely. As per TRAI regulations, you can modify your channel selection, upgrade or downgrade packages, and add or drop individual a-la-carte channels at any time. Changes made before the 15th of the month are prorated for the current billing cycle. You can manage your channels through the set-top box menu, our customer portal, or by calling support.

Is there a warranty on the set-top box and equipment?

Yes. All set-top boxes and equipment provided by Add On Communications come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty against manufacturing defects. This covers internal hardware, remote control, and the power adapter. Physical damage, water damage, or accidental damage is not covered under warranty. After the warranty period, we offer paid repair or replacement options with a 90-day service warranty on the repaired unit.
