Understanding TRAI NCF Charges

As per the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) regulations, all cable TV and DTH subscribers are required to pay a Network Capacity Fee (NCF) in addition to the subscription charges for selected channels and bouquets.

The NCF structure ensures fair pricing and gives you the freedom to choose only the channels you want to watch. Here's how it works:

  • ₹130 as NCF for the first 200 channels (including taxes)
  • ₹2 per additional channel beyond 200 channels
  • Maximum NCF cap of ₹160 per month for unlimited channels
  • 18% GST applicable on NCF as per government regulations

NCF is billed separately from your channel subscription and is standard across all service providers in India. This transparent pricing model is designed to protect consumer interests and promote healthy competition in the broadcast industry.
