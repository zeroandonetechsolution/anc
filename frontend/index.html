SUPPORT & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I choose Add On Communications over other cable TV providers?

Add On Communications combines a 12-year legacy in cable distribution with next-gen 4K smart hardware and fiber infrastructure. Our 500+ LCO partners give us unmatched on-ground service, while enterprise-grade headend technology from Cisco and Evertz delivers 99.9% uptime, 24x7 support and over 600 channels to your home.

What is an MSO and how does Add On Communications operate as one?

An MSO (Multi-System Operator) acquires content from broadcasters and aggregates it at a central headend, then distributes it through a network of local cable operators (LCOs) to subscribers. Add On Communications manages the core fiber backbone, headend operations and set top box ecosystem, while our LCO partners handle last-mile delivery and on-site service in their respective territories.

What is AOD and how does it benefit subscribers?

AOD stands for Add-On-Demand. It is a TRAI-mandated framework that allows you to build a customised channel bouquet by combining a base package with individual a-la-carte channels or small packs. With Add On Communications you can pick and choose exactly what you want to watch using our Selfcare portal, ensuring you only pay for the content you actually consume.

We will verify service availability in your locality within 2 hours and schedule a free installation slot that suits you. Most installations are completed within 24-48 hours of booking.

Is Add On Communications's service scalable for businesses and enterprises?

Absolutely. We offer dedicated enterprise plans for hotels, hospitals, educational institutions, apartment complexes and corporate offices. Solutions include bulk set top box deployments, custom channel bouquets, dedicated bandwidth, IPTV distribution over LAN and 24x7 priority SLA-backed support. Contact our enterprise team for a custom proposal.

Do your cable operators also offer broadband and fiber internet?

Yes. Many of our 500+ LCO partners offer fiber broadband over the same local network, powered by Add On Communications's core backbone. Speeds range from 50 Mbps to 1 Gbps with unlimited data options. You can bundle broadband with your cable TV package for significant savings and a single unified bill.

Can I access popular OTT apps through my Add On Communications set top box?

Our Smart 4K set top box ships with native support for Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, ZEE5, SonyLIV, YouTube and many more. You can sign in to your existing OTT accounts directly from the box and enjoy all your streaming subscriptions alongside live cable TV through a single unified interface.

Does Add On Communications provide HD and 4K channels?

Yes. We currently deliver 80+ HD channels across all major genres and languages as part of our HD and Smart 4K plans, plus selected 4K UHD channels and on-demand 4K content through our OTT integrations. A compatible HD or 4K set top box is required to experience these higher resolutions.
